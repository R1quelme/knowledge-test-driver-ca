Drive Test does not directly collect or store any personal information. The app does not require you to create an account or provide any personal data to us.

The app stores your study progress (which questions you have seen) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Drive Test offers optional in-app purchases to unlock premium features. All payments are processed by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play Store. We do not collect or store any payment information. Please review the privacy policies of Apple and Google for details on how they handle payment data.

Third-Party Services

The app may use the following third-party services:

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Advertising shown in the app is configured to be non-personalized for all users.
